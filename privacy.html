Privacy Policy

Hangul a Day · Last updated October 8, 2026

Hangul a Day publishes free Korean-learning videos to its own Instagram, Facebook and YouTube accounts. This policy explains what information our software handles.

Who uses the app

The "Hangul a Day" app is used only by its owner to publish videos to the owner's own channels. It is not offered to the public and does not let anyone else sign in.

Information we access

With the owner's permission, the app uses Google's YouTube Data API (scope youtube.upload) and Meta's APIs only to upload and publish our own lesson videos, with their titles and descriptions, to our own accounts. We do not read, collect or store data about viewers or other users, and we do not access anyone's personal Google, YouTube, Instagram or Facebook data.

How credentials are stored

Access tokens for our own accounts are stored encrypted in a private repository and used only to publish our videos. They are never shared or sold.

Sharing

We do not sell or share personal information. Videos are published publicly on the platforms above, subject to those platforms' own privacy policies (Google, Meta).

YouTube API Services

Our use of YouTube API Services is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. You can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
